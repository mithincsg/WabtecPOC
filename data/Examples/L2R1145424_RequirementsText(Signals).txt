18 Signals 
 L2R1145424 
When the on-board segment generates one of the following targets 
• a head-end only restricted speed becoming located signal target or 
• a head-end only restricted speed unknown signal target or 
• a head-end only restricted speed change in train orientation signal target and 
that target starts at the beginning of the train’s calculated route where the country of operation is not Canada, 
the on-board segment shall set the speed of that target to TBC137 mph using the value of the operating railroad 
of the track at the near target limit.
