15 Speed Enforcement 
15.1 Speed Restriction Targets 
15.1.1 Track Based Speed Restrictions 

L2R9479 
When the on-board segment generates a target for a track based subdivision / district level speed restriction or 
block level speed restriction of restriction type "generic", "tonnage", "TPOB", or "axle count" and the track 
database indicates that the speed is restricted speed, the on-board segment shall set the target speed to the 
most restrictive of: 
• the speed defined in the track database or 
• the value of TBC137 defined in the railroad specific configuration fileset of the operating railroad of 
the subdivision/district.
