13 Switch Enforcement 
13.1 Unmonitored Switches on PTC Track over which Movement is 
Governed by a Signal or Monitored Indirectly by Track Circuit 
13.1.1 Facing Approach 
2.     L2R7983 
When an unmonitored switch is on the train’s calculated route and 
• that switch is greater than the calculated position uncertainty in advance of the leading edge of the 
train and 
• the train’s calculated route indicates a facing approach to that switch and 
• the track within one foot in approach to the switch point of that switch is PTC track and 
• the on-board segment does not have a stored “suspended” status for that switch and 
• the facing approach to that switch is designated in the track data as  
o “protected by track circuit, stop required” or 
o “protected by track circuit, no stop required” and 
• that switch is not located in any restricted speed target of any type other than  
o a head-end only restricted speed TBC137 mph becoming located signal target or 
o a head-end only restricted speed TBC137 mph route based cab signal target with an 
enforcement delay greater than zero (0) seconds or 
o a head-end only restricted speed TBC137 mph speed enforcement cab signal target with an 
enforcement delay greater than zero (0) seconds and 
• that switch is not located in any head-end only zero (0) mph unknown signal target and 
• that switch is not located in any head-end only zero (0) mph signal target and 
• that switch is not located in any head-end only zero (0) mph enter main track target and 
• that switch is not located in any head-end only zero (0) mph pass signal at stop target and 
• that switch is not located in any head-end only zero (0) mph reverse movement target and 
• the “Indicate Switch Alignment” key has not been pressed for that switch since the switch appeared on 
the train’s calculated route greater than the calculated position uncertainty in advance of the leading 
edge of the train and 
• the locomotive track location and calculated direction of movement were known prior to the leading 
edge of the train coming within calculated position uncertainty of the track circuit in which the switch is 
located, 
the on-board segment shall utilize the NORMAL switch position for generating the train’s calculated route.
