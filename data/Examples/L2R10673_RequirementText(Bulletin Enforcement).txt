When the on-board segment has a calculated route for the train and 

the on-board segment has a dataset for a work zone that is within TBC290 seconds of becoming effective or is already in effect and 
CFG22 is disabled for the operating railroad that issued the dataset,
the on-board segment shall generate a separate entire train zero (0) mph work zone target for each set of non-contiguous limits 

that are defined in the dataset and 
that are on the train’s calculated route and 
that are on PTC track and
that are not within the limits of any entire train maximum authorized speed PTC suspension target and
where the far limit is in advance of the trailing edge of the train or less than the calculated position uncertainty behind the trailing edge of the train and
which are not already covered by a target for the same work zone dataset.
